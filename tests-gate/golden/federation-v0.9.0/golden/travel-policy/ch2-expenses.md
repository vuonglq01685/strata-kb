## 2.1 Expense classes

Three classes: A transport via preferred vendor, B lodging within the city cap, C receipted meals up to the daily cap. Anything else is Class X and needs director approval before the trip.

## 2.2 Booking window

Book domestic trips at least 7 days ahead and international trips at least 21 days ahead. Inside-window bookings need a recorded manager reason. Post-ticket changes are charged to the requesting team.
