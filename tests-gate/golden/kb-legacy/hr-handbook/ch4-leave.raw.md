## 4.10 Annual leave

Annual leave accrues from the first day of continuous employment. Full-time staff accrue 20 days per calendar year. Part-time staff accrue pro rata against contracted hours. Unused days carry forward to a cap of 10 days; any remainder above the cap is paid out in the January payroll. A request is submitted through the HR portal at least 10 working days before the first day of leave, except where the manager agrees a shorter notice in writing.

The calendar year for accrual is 1 January to 31 December, regardless of the employee's start date. A starter in mid-year receives a pro-rata slice of the 20-day allowance, rounded up to the next half day. Leavers are paid for unused accrued days in the final payroll; days already taken beyond the pro-rata slice are recovered from that payroll. Public holidays that fall inside a booked block are not deducted from the annual-leave balance. Managers must answer a portal request within three working days; a missed deadline auto-escalates to HR operations.

Shift workers accrue the same 20-day allowance converted to hours using the contracted weekly hours divided by five. Taking leave on a scheduled 12-hour shift deducts 12 hours, not 8. The portal shows the hour balance, not a day count, for anyone on a non-standard roster.

## 4.11 Sick leave

Sick leave is paid at full salary for up to 10 working days per calendar year. A medical certificate is required from the third consecutive day. Days taken as sick leave do not reduce the annual-leave balance. Abuse of sick leave, determined after a documented review, is a disciplinary matter under chapter 9.

The 10-day allowance resets every 1 January and does not carry forward. Partial days are allowed in half-day units when the employee works the other half. A certificate must name the employee, the date of examination, and the period covered; a fit-for-work date is optional. HR may request a further certificate when an absence exceeds ten consecutive working days, even if the annual allowance still has days left. Recurring short absences on Mondays or Fridays trigger a welfare conversation, not an automatic disciplinary file.

## 4.12 Parental leave

An employee becomes eligible for paid parental leave after 12 months of continuous service. Entitlement is calculated per the table below. The 12-month clock restarts after any break in service longer than 30 calendar days. Leave may be taken in one block or split into at most two blocks, each of at least two weeks, provided both blocks fall within 12 months of the child's birth or placement.

| Service       | Paid weeks | Unpaid weeks |
|---------------|------------|--------------|
| 12–24 months  | 8          | 4            |
| 24+ months    | 14         | 4            |

Partners who are Acme employees may take the same unpaid weeks concurrently. Paid weeks cannot be transferred between partners. Payroll loads the paid portion in the cycle after HR confirms the dates.

Notice is eight weeks before the intended start, with a birth certificate or placement letter attached. When the birth is earlier than planned, notice is as soon as practicable and the eight-week rule is waived. Keeping-in-touch days, up to ten, may be worked during the leave without ending it; they are paid at the ordinary rate and do not extend the leave. Returning employees have the right to the same role or a role of equivalent grade and pay. A request to return part-time for three months is considered in good faith and refused only with a written operational reason.

## 4.13 Restrictive leave

Restrictive leave is unpaid leave that a manager may grant when annual leave is exhausted and the absence is not covered by sick leave or parental leave. The cap is 20 working days in any rolling 12-month window. Consecutive restrictive leave longer than 10 working days requires director approval. Restrictive leave does not accrue, does not carry forward, and does not count as service for parental-leave eligibility.

A request names the dates, the reason in one paragraph, and the cover plan. The manager decides within five working days. Director approval, when needed, is recorded in the same portal ticket before the first day of absence. Restrictive leave overlapping a public holiday is still unpaid for that holiday. An employee on a performance plan may be refused restrictive leave when the absence would block a scheduled review; that refusal is written, not verbal. Days on restrictive leave do not break continuity of employment for unfair-dismissal qualifying time, even though they do not count toward parental-leave service.
